# metric — quantomize: the corpus measured as one quantum metric

The session's measurements are **classical** — one number each, read one at a time: [[session]] build/heal, [[gravity]] concentration, [[leftover]] residual, [[rosetta]] gravityPools. `quantomize` makes them **quantum** in the corpus's sense ([[think]]`.superpose`): every reading held **at once**, folded to one address, and **coherent** iff no two readings of the same name contradict. The whole state of the corpus reads as one.

## There is a real science, and this is not (quite) it

**Quantum metrology** is real — using entangled states to beat the standard quantum limit toward the **Heisenberg limit** in precision measurement. This is **not** that. Here *"quantum"* is the superposition overlay ([[quantum]]/gaps): readings held together and read as one, coherent when they agree. It is a **structural snapshot**, not a precision-measurement scheme, and I will not dress it as one.

## Honest boundary — coherence is agreement, not truth

A quantum metric is **coherent** (no two same-named readings disagree), never **true** — the readings can each be wrong; coherence means only that they do not contradict **each other**. Tested: three broken zeros are perfectly coherent, and all wrong. A **decohering** metric — two readings of one name, different values — is the tell that a measurement is being reported two ways, the same decoherence [[think]] uses for thought, turned on the instruments. That decoherence is genuinely useful: it catches a metric that disagrees with itself (a `residual` reported as both `1113` and `0`). **HARMONY ≠ TRUTH, even for the ruler.**

**Law — [[law]]: the metrics are quantum too — `quantomize` holds every reading at once in one coherent superposition, folded to a single address; coherent iff no instrument disagrees with itself, decohering where one metric is reported two ways. Coherence is agreement between instruments, never truth — three broken zeros cohere and are all wrong; a quantum metric is a consistent snapshot, not a correct one.**

## Standards

- **Quantum metrology (Heisenberg limit)** — the real field, named to mark the boundary; this atom is the structural analogy, not the scheme.
- **Popper** — coherent readings are not verified by cohering; a consistent snapshot can be uniformly wrong.
