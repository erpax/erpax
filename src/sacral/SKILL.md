It is a **standalone projection** of [[chakra]]: the seven centers are computed once by `chakras()` — each [[horo]] position decoded to a note, a colour and a coordinate from the SAME content-uuid — and this atom merely READS index 1. It never recomputes the math.

The computed CMYK colour is **NOT claimed identical** to the traditional rainbow orange; the rainbow is a cultural overlay carried alongside the computed signal, not asserted to match it.

This atom asserts nothing about a body or an energy field; only position → note → uuid is computed. Ground it honestly in [[law]]: a center earns its place by zero-entropy uuid-wiring, not by tradition. As a center it sits one step from the [[heart]] (index 3) on the same ring.
