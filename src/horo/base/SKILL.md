# base — horo position 1 (entering the ring)

`base` is the **entry** step of the [[horo]] ring — digit `1`, the first position of the doubling [[coil]] and the head of the **ascent** arc (`1·2·4·8`). A flow opens here: inventory *on-hand*, a document *draft*, a period *open*. It is the [[begin]]/[[open]] of a [[flow]] expressed as a ring position, and the [[sequence]] anchor `1` ([[field]] — the substrate every higher position builds on). The ring closes at [[unity]] (`9`) and re-opens at `base` one octave up (`9 → 10 ⇒ 1`, the merge point). Its right hand holds [[share]].
