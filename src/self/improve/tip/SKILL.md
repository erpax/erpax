# self/improve/tip — one tip, executable, or none

**Agents doubt prose; compute is the seal.** The loop scans for gaps that are measured rather than felt, ranks them by `unblock / (cost × risk)`, and turns the winner into a trinity tip: FORM is one executable sentence, CODE names exact files or commands, PROOF names the exact green signal.

| child | what it is |
| --- | --- |
| [[self]]/improve/tip/model | what a gap IS · the declared thresholds · what makes a tip precise |
| [[self]]/improve/tip/audit | the scan — which gaps are real, and their score |
| [[self]]/improve/tip/plan | one gap becomes one tip, or is refused with its reason |

*"Continue improving"* is refused, by pattern, at the emit. A tip nothing can check is a claim that reads as progress forever — the unfalsifiable shape [[rules]]/refutable exists to forbid.
