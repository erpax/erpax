# self/improve/tip/model — what a gap is, and what makes a tip precise

The shared vocabulary of the loop. A `SelfDevGap` is a named blockage with a cost, a risk and an unblock value; a `TrinityTip` is FORM (one executable sentence) ⊗ CODE (exact files or commands) ⊗ PROOF (the exact green signal).

The three regexes are the loop's own gate. `VAGUE_TIP_RE` matches the things that read like a plan and forbid nothing — *"continue improving"*, *"look into"* — and `CONCRETE_CODE_RE` / `CONCRETE_PROOF_RE` demand a real command and a real signal. A tip nothing can check is the unfalsifiable claim [[rules]]/refutable forbids, wearing a plan's clothes.

The thresholds are DECLARED (`ADMIN_TTFB_MS_OK`, `ADMIN_TTFB_MS_RESIDUAL`), in the open, so they can be argued with rather than inferred from whatever the corpus happened to measure last.
