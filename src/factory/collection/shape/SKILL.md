# factory/collection/shape — a new table needs a new shape

`collectionSignature` scores a collection against the closed nine-axis basis (money · party · item · period · lifecycle · record · schedule · report · standard). The law it serves: **a new collection is warranted only by a NEW signature** — otherwise what you have is a row, not a table.

`shapeRatchetVerdict` fails closed against `ROSETTA_BASELINE`, so the basis cannot quietly grow; the ceiling ratchets DOWN in the same diff that folds a collection away. `auditCorpus` reports the collapse clusters — the candidates, never the decision.

**Honest boundary.** A signature is scored against a DECLARED marker list, so it reports what its basis can see — a frozen rosetta reports what its author remembered, which is why the ratchet exists and why a merge is decided by a human and only ever proposed here ([[rules]]/collapse).
