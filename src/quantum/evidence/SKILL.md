# evidence — a claim is held at the status it has earned, not the one it is delivered in

Research arrives flattened. A survey presents a settled theorem, a contested reading, a plain error and a bare metaphor in the same voice, at the same confidence, and the reader has no way to tell them apart — because **fiction and truth read identically** ([[rules]]/prose). Flattened confidence is the carrier, not the content.

This atom refuses the flattening. Every claim is stored with three things it usually loses:

| field | why it must be stored |
| --- | --- |
| `verdict` | a theorem and a metaphor are not the same kind of thing and must not be shelved together |
| `refutedBy` | the observation that would make it FALSE — the only thing that makes it a claim at all |
| `boundary` | where the claim stops, so a true statement is not read wider than it holds |

## The one invariant

**An empirical claim with no refutation condition fails closed.** Not a ratchet — zero. `unfalsifiable` and `metaphor` are exempt *only by declaring their own emptiness*, which is the honest state rather than the defect. What the gate catches is the claim that reads as empirical and quietly cannot be contradicted: the space where a lie is safe ([[rules]]/refutable).

`open` is kept distinct from `contested` on purpose. `open` is genuine superposition — uncollapsed. `contested` is evidence pulling both ways. Merging them would hide which claims are waiting for a measurement and which are waiting for a decision.

## Agnostic means the source is not evidence

Claims are stated as propositions about the world, never as *"the source says."* Attribution is provenance; it is not a reason to believe. A test asserts no claim smuggles authority back in as its ground — because authority is never a step ([[theorem]]).

That also means an `error` verdict costs nothing to record. Three sit in the body today: a delayed-choice eraser read as changing the past (fringes exist only in coincidence-sorted subsets, never in the raw pattern), transistor switching attributed to tunnelling (it is field-effect; tunnelling is the leakage limit), and a 1961 two-slit experiment credited with single-particle emission (it used a beam; the single-particle builds came in 1976 and 1989). Each is stored beside what refutes it, so the correction travels with the claim.

## Declared and computed, split in the open

The **verdicts are DECLARED** — no theorem assigns an epistemic status to an empirical claim; a human reads the evidence and writes it where it can be argued with. That is the same split [[rules]]/audience makes for its role→standard map, and stating it plainly is what keeps a hand-picked list from posing as a measurement.

What is **COMPUTED**: the distribution across verdicts, the refutability check, and `evidenceUuid` — so reordering or editing any claim changes the address, and a revised body cannot silently replace the old one.

**Honest boundary.** This proves a claim **carries a refutation condition**, never that the condition is the **right** one, nor that the verdict is correct — a wrong verdict stated refutably is exactly what this is for, because it can be argued with. It records status; it does not adjudicate science.

**Law — [[law]]: a claim is stored with what would refute it, or it is not stored as a claim. Verdict, refutation and boundary travel together — flatten them and a metaphor shelves beside a theorem, which is how the reader is lost.**
