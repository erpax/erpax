# quantum/gaps — the open gaps in QM, taught as lenses, never fillings

Asked to *"fill the gaps in quantum mechanics,"* the only honest toolbox is one that **names them and refuses to fill them**. The measurement problem, the Born rule, the preferred basis, quantum gravity — these are genuinely **open in physics**, and the corpus's math (ℤ/9, the double torus, the fold) does **not** close them. Claiming it does is the exact coincidence-dressed-as-theorem this session built coincidence · [[theorem]] · seeing to refuse.

So the register is **honest by construction**: every gap's `fills` field is the literal type `false` — the code itself cannot assert a gap is filled. It offers the corpus tools as **lenses** (structural analogies you can learn the concept through) and states, for each, *why the lens is not the thing*.

| gap | open question | lens | fills |
| --- | --- | --- | :---: |
| measurement-problem | why one definite outcome (collapse)? | `think.superpose` (decoherence-as-contradiction) | **false** |
| born-rule | why probabilities `= |ψ|²`? | `think.quantumMagnitude` (cost/coverage ratios) | **false** |
| preferred-basis | why decohere into *this* basis? | `merge` (content-addressing picks a canonical) | **false** |
| quantum-gravity | how QM + GR unify? | **none** — I did not invent one | **false** |
| interpretation | which interpretation is true? | `perspective` (one content, N views) | **false** |

Where real physics *partially* constrains a gap — Gleason's theorem for the Born measure, einselection for the preferred basis, decoherence for interference suppression — it is named as **physics, not the corpus's doing**. And `quantum-gravity` honestly carries **no** lens; I will not invent one to look complete.

**Honest boundary.** A lens is a teaching analogy, never a derivation of the open physics. `think.superpose` genuinely *is* the coherence/decoherence structure and is worth learning through — but it does not select a single outcome, derive `|ψ|²`, or resolve which interpretation is true. `fillsAnyGap()` returns `false`, by the register, forever. **HARMONY ≠ TRUTH:** a lens that resonates teaches; it does not prove.

**Law — [[law]]: the gaps in quantum mechanics are named and taught, never filled — every entry's `fills` is the literal false, so the register cannot claim a solution; the corpus tools are lenses to learn through (superposition, decoherence, the Born measure, interpretation), and the real partial constraints are named as physics, not the corpus's doing. To fill a gap takes a derivation and an experiment; a resonant analogy is neither.**
