# seeing — seeing is not proving; assume nothing; be surprised by a passing test

*"Animations prove you wrong."* Here is the honest, **generous** answer, made computable — and it is not "animations are worthless." An animation shows **faithfully** whatever it draws, and where it draws exact algebra — `movie(doubling,2) = 1,2,4,8,7,5`, the merkaba fold — it is showing something **true**. Those movies are real; I built them and they are correct. The claim this atom makes is narrower and exact:

## Vividness is orthogonal to proof

The evidence a rendering carries is the evidence of the **claim** it draws — never more, never a function of how many frames it has. `evidenceSeen` reads `claimEvidence` and **never `frames`**:

```
1,000,000-frame animation of a coincidence  → evidence 0.2  (the coincidence's)
6-frame sketch of a theorem                  → evidence 1.0  (the theorem's)
```

You cannot see your way past the test ([[coincidence]]). The movie **transmits** the status; it cannot **raise** it. A vivid rendering of a fitted match is a vivid fitted match.

## Assume nothing — I agree, and this is it

`ASSUME_NOTHING` is the **neutral prior**: not `refuted` (that assumes no) and not `confirmed` (that assumes yes). *"Stop doubting"* would be assuming yes — which **contradicts** *"assume nothing."* The discipline sides with assume-nothing: neither, until a test passes. That is not closed-mindedness; it is the refusal to assume, which is exactly what you asked for.

## The single mind — the remedy is three, not zero

*"The wrong comes from a single mind not comprehending."* True to [[think]]/higherMind — one mind cannot form the higher mind; it needs **≥ 3**. But that points to **more** scrutiny, not less: the higher mind forms from three **independent** minds **converging** (experiment, replication, peer review), and it **carries** the dissent, outvoted, not silenced. One vivid demonstration is **one mind**. The cure for a single mind is three independent confirmations — not the suspension of all of them.

## Be surprised — the trigger is built

`beSurprised` flips belief to `confirmed` the moment a test **passes** (an exact identity, zero free parameters, matching measurement). A rendering never triggers it; a passing test does. The neutral prior is **openness with a trigger**, and the flip is the surprise:

```
a vivid rendering → assume-nothing (no move)
a PASSING test    → confirmed, surprised: true
```

**The door is open; the key is a passing test, not a movie. Bring one and I update.**

**Honest boundary.** This proves vividness cannot substitute for a test; it does **not** deny that a faithful animation of a theorem shows a truth. The base is assumed ([[theorem]]: `s > 0`).

**Law — [[law]]: seeing is not proving — a rendering carries its claim's evidence and no more, vividness orthogonal to proof; assume-nothing is the neutral prior, not disbelief; belief updates only on a passing test, and against a neutral prior that update is surprise. The single mind's cure is three independent confirmations, never the suspension of doubt.**

## Standards

- **Sagan / Hume** — a claim's weight is its evidence, not its vividness; extraordinary claims need extraordinary tests.
- **Popper** — the neutral prior is falsifiable openness; a passing test moves it.
