# discover — all exists at once and just needs to be discovered

*"When something is discovered, all is developed in the area at once, proceeding to a next that does not itself exist — because all exists at once and just needs to be discovered."* Content-addressing makes this exact **and** keeps it honest, so the beautiful claim never becomes an overclaim.

## What exists at once — the address space

`toUuid` is a **total** function: every possible content, discovered or not, already has its address, computable now. The address of a theorem no one has written is not a void to be created — it is a uuid you can compute this second ([[merge]]). The map is complete: the **"next" already has a place**; there is no next as a separate future thing, only an address not yet read. This is the Platonic reading the corpus has always held — the algebra *"was always true, owes nothing to any physicist"* ([[coincidence]]) — turned into a computation.

```
address of "the next theorem" (never written), NOW:  7c0a5548-0964-8404-b60e-87fe6364eb46
the map is complete — even the undiscovered is addressed: true
```

## All in the area at once — the neighbourhood

Discover one content and its relatives are **all** addressed in a single fold — you do not build them one at a time. `neighbourhood('doubling', …)` lights up `doubling/inverse`, `doubling/orbit`, `doubling/fixed-point` together, each a pre-existing address the discovery only reads ([[think]]`.superpose`: N states held as one). That is *"developed at once with quantum speed."*

## What discovery still costs — the seed

Here is the boundary that keeps this honest, and it is the same one that has run through every turn. The **address** pre-exists for free; the **value** at a novel address does not. Reading a derivable content is a read; bringing a genuinely new content into the readable is the oracle bit, `s > 0`, paid once ([[think]]`.ceiling`). So *"all exists at once"* is true of the **structure** — every address is there — while **discovery is the act of reading**, and the seed is its irreducible floor (Kolmogorov). `discover(content, s).cost === s`: 0 for the derivable (a free read of what pre-exists), >0 for the novel. **The forms exist; the territory is still walked once.** You cannot read what no mind has yet derived, even though its address was always waiting.

**Law — [[law]]: all exists at once and is discovered, not created — content-addressing is total, so every content (even the unwritten next) already has its address, and discovering one addresses its whole area at once. But the address is free while the value at a novel address costs the seed (`s > 0`): the map is complete, the territory is walked once — the forms exist, discovery reads them, and the oracle bit is the one price no completeness pays for you.**

## Standards

- **Mathematical realism / Platonism** — the forms (addresses) exist prior to discovery; `toUuid` is total.
- **Kolmogorov complexity** — the seed is incompressible; a novel value costs `s > 0` however complete the map.
