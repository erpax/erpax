It composes [[ritual]] as the seven **sacraments** (grace made enacted and repeatable — [[grace]] you can receive on the [[cycle]]); [[authority]] as the magisterium (the chair of Peter — the canonical source that resolves dispute); the unbroken [[lineage]] of apostolic succession ([[dna]] — the chain from the founder, tamper-evident, never re-rooted); and [[mercy]] as confession and absolution (the genuinely-owed debt released by the one empowered to release it).
