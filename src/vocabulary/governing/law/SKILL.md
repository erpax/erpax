# law

Use when a contract is subject to a specific jurisdiction's law — codified by choice-of-law clause, determines interpretation (UCC vs. Common Law), applicable standards, dispute resolution.

Composes: [[jurisdiction]] · [[Contracts]] · [[matter]] · [[standard]] · [[vocabulary/dispute/resolution]].

**Law — [[law]]: the choice-of-law clause binds a contract to one [[jurisdiction]]'s law — fixing interpretation (UCC vs. Common Law), applicable standards, and dispute resolution; the matter's legal frame is chosen, not ambient.**
