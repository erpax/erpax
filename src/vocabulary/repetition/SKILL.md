# repetition — the model of one [[repetitions]] row

One repeated occurrence within a counted set. The singular model whose plural store is the [[repetitions]] collection ([[balance]]: every collection has its model).

Composes [[repetitions]] · [[exercise]] · [[balance]].

**Law — [[law]]: one repetition is the singular model of exactly one [[repetitions]] row — every collection has its model ([[balance]]), so the plural store and its singular type are two halves of one node.**
