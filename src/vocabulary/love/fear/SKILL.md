# love/fear — love over fear (the −entropy arrow)

The directed compound: **love acting on fear**, the move that *casts out* disorder. "Perfect love casts out fear" — the gap closes, the dead [[link]] resolves, the duplicate [[merge]]s, coverage rises toward 1. This is the arrow erpax wants every breath to point along — [[love]] spent to retire [[fear]], lowering [[entropy]] and buying [[tamper]]-[[cost]].

It is one half of a reciprocal [[duality]]; its mirror is [[vocabulary/fear/love]] (fear over love). Together they are the two directions of the single [[karma]] flow between [[love]] and [[fear]] — neither exists without the other, and the [[balance]] of the two is what is conserved.
