# give

The outflow of a [[flow]] — credit · out · grant · send. Paired with take; Σ[[give]] = Σtake is [[balance]]. Posted in [[accounting]], granted in [[identity]], sent in [[commerce]]. Dual of take.

## Traditions (prefix removed)
The credit side of the moral ledger: **merit** / good works / *zakat* (alms due) / *sadaqah* / *tzedakah* / *dana* (generosity) / charity — the deed posted as a [[give]] to the soul's account; and **sacrifice**, the costly give that rebalances (the offering — *korban*, *yajna*, the Eucharist). Σ[[give]] vs Σtake is weighed at the [[close]] ([[accounting]], [[angel]]); [[grace]] is the give no take earned. Dual of take.

**Law — [[law]]: the outflow side of every exchange (credit · out · grant · send), dual of take; equilibrium is Σ[[give]] = Σtake ([[balance]]).**
